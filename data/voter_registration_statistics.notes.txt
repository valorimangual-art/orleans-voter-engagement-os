# Statistics Analysis Notes:
	The following are notes on how to approach the data in the precinct statistics spreadsheet.
	The usage patterns are irrespective of any selected group and should be applied to any subset of the data

## Interface Notes:
	* The columns on the main tab (precincts) are auto-filtered - clicking on the dropdown icon will provide filtering options
	* The pivot tables (tabs labeled by_xxx) are interactive
	** Clicking on a cell will create a new spreadsheet containing the underlying data
	** The group rows can be filtered and the tables totals will automatically update

## Usage Pattern for Voter Registration
	The primary metric for efficiently canvassing unregistered voters is the absolute number of *unregisted voters*!
	Sort by total number of unregistered voters and then examine the registration rate.
	An area that is high in absolute numbers of *unregistered* voters and has low registration rates increases the chance that a given door is an unregistered voter.
	Note that the bias of cultural factors need to be removed! For example Tulane has a high unregistered number, but it also has a population (students) that are ineligible!

## Usage Pattern for Voter Activation
	The primary metric for efficiently canvassing *registered* voters -- ie: voter mobilization
	Sort by total number of registered voters and then examine the registration rate.
	An area that is high in absolute numbers of registered voters and has high registration rates increases the chance that a given door is a registered voter.
